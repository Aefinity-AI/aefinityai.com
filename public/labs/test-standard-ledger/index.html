01Why it matters

Results are only as credible as the process behind them.

02Method, step by step

  1. Write the pass mark before the run.
  2. Publish a salted, hash-chained fingerprint of the private register.
  3. Number every defect as an erratum.
  4. Remove numbers that have no provenance.

Method as recorded

Stated practices in public files: (1) pre-registration, a pass mark written before the run, then a verdict; (2) a public salted, hash-chained fingerprint ledger of the private register, with a verify_ledger.py script and an OpenTimestamps proof file (LEDGER.tsv.ots); (3) no timing or throughput numbers in the spec repo; (4) provenance on every number; (5) defects recorded as numbered errata in CHANGELOG.md, and pinned digests do not move silently; (6) mismatches are welcomed as results. The standard is stated and applied by us, not audited by an outside party. All six URLs returned HTTP 200 unauthenticated (github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com) on 2026-10-12.

03Results, exact and sourced

  1. R1The ledger covers 84 registered experiments, 65 with a verdict; 161 of 233 lines are backfill.

  2. R2The ledger states what it cannot prove.

  3. R3The start rule is a promise the page itself cannot check.

  4. R4Provenance on every number, or the number is removed.

  5. R5Pinned digests do not move silently.

  6. R6No unqualified superlatives.

  7. R7A mismatch is a result.

04What it does NOT show

That we always followed the standard; no outsider has audited it.

05Scope and caveats

  • Stated and applied by us; no unaffiliated audit of whether practice always matched the statement.
  • 161 of 233 ledger lines are backfill and prove existence only from their publication date; backfill lines show no order of writing.
  • The ledger shows counts and line kinds, not what was tested or found.
  • The ledger count is a snapshot and will grow.
  • The ledger cannot show when an experiment ran; the rule that a run starts only after its bar line is public applies from the day of publication on, with exceptions marked start-unanchored.
  • The ledger chain verification was not re-run by this verifier; the earlier claim of an unauthenticated-clone verification is removed from the method text.

06Outcome

Mixed

Some parts held, some did not, or we corrected ourselves.

07What we learned

We publish how we test, including what the method cannot prove, and we correct our own published claims through numbered errata.
