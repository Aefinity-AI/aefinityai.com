02Method, step by step

  1. Publish a table for verified reproductions and divergences.

Method as recorded

A public table of verified reproductions and verified divergences for the CIS-2 spec (v0.3b). Credit only, no cash or prize. Only runs with a full log are listed. Reporters can also open a clean-room implementation PR or a spec-defect issue (CONTRIBUTING.md).

03Results, exact and sourced

  1. R1Every listed reproduction is our own run (two internal rows, 2026-09-22, own machines: Intel i5-5200U with AVX2 and Intel Celeron N4020 scalar, each 9 passed / 0 failed). No third-party reproduction is listed.

  2. R2No divergence has been reported as of this check (2026-10-12).

04What it does NOT show

Anything about whether outsiders will succeed. Our own two rows only seed the table's format.

05Scope and caveats

  • A standing challenge, not a completed experiment.
  • No unaffiliated reproduction is recorded.
  • The two internal rows are run by us on our own machines (Debian 13, Linux 6.12.94); the source says they only seed the table's format.
  • The logs for both internal rows are not committed to the public repo; the page says a public log commit is a follow-up.
  • The quote wraps across a line break in the source (whitespace only).

06Outcome

Null

No effect found, or nothing yet to report.

07What we learned

As of 2026-10-12 no one outside the lab has reproduced or broken the CIS-2 results, and the page says so plainly. The only listed runs are our own, and their logs are not yet public.
