A.L.I.C.E. · Bare-metal inference

Bare-metal inference, built for air-gapped work.

A.L.I.C.E. is inference built to run without a general-purpose operating system, using integer and ternary math. Every generation carries a receipt that a separate host verifier re-derives. Built for air-gapped inference and security, and for defense and Army field requirements.

Book a briefing →A.L.I.C.E. on GitHub
  • Primary use: inference and security on air-gapped networks
  • Built to run without a general-purpose operating system
  • No outside data processor, no cloud connection required
  • Integer and ternary arithmetic (CIS-1), designed for bit-identical results
  • Today: BitNet b1.58 2B-4T, vocabulary-pruned, on ordinary x86 CPUs

How it works

  1. 1

    Bootable image

    Inference runs without a general-purpose operating system underneath it.

  2. 2

    Integer inference

    A ternary BitNet b1.58 model, integer arithmetic, on the CPU.

  3. 3

    Receipt

    Each generation produces a receipt.

  4. 4

    Host verifier

    A separate verifier re-derives the receipt and reports any mismatch.

Evidence

What runs today

Everything below is from our own runs. No outside party has reproduced any of it yet.

  • Model

    BitNet b1.58 2B-4T, vocabulary-pruned, run under CIS-1 integer math.

  • Receipts

    In October 2026, one generation produced the same receipt on three x86 machines, with and without AVX2, and a standalone verifier re-verified it on each.

  • Bootable image

    Demonstrated in QEMU and on physical hardware with a smaller model.

Target use cases

Primary use: air-gapped inference and security.

  • Defense, government and secure facilities

    Restricted networks are disconnected on purpose.

    Inference and security work on disconnected networks, field kits and edge devices, on hardware you control. Built for defense and Army field requirements; we are pursuing Army SBIR work.

  • Critical infrastructure and industrial sites

    Operations cannot depend on the network.

    An offline operations assistant for procedures, logs and shift handovers that keeps working when the network does not.

  • Remote and field operations

    Ships, remote sites, disaster response.

    A working assistant aboard a ship, at a remote site or in a response kit, with no connectivity required.

Also suited to

  • Hospitals and clinics

    Patient data cannot leave the hospital network.

    Clinical notes, discharge letters, inbox triage and coding support, on machines inside the hospital network. Designed to keep patient data on hospital hardware.

  • Law firms and legal departments

    Privileged documents cannot go to a third-party processor.

    Review and summarize privileged documents with no third-party processor.

  • Finance and insurance

    Decisions are examined after the fact.

    Decision support, document review and model risk checks on your own servers.

  • Research and regulated R&D

    IP has to stay in house.

    Keep IP and AI-assisted work in house, on your own machines.

  • Any team handling sensitive data

    HR, internal investigations, board materials.

    Summarize and search HR files, investigation notes and board papers on a machine in the room, designed so nothing leaves it.
